## Reflection

1. **The three most frustrating or time-consuming parts:** (1) Looking up the predicted lineups on Ligainsider, (2) checking market values on Kickbase, and (3) checking whether the player I want is on the Kickbase transfer market.
2. **What would make me trust a system's recommendation? What would make me ignore it?** I would trust it if its source is trustworthy. I would not trust it if the source is not serious.
3. **What a system should never do on my behalf without asking:** Blindly trust Kickbase market values.
4. **How I would know it was actually helping** (this becomes my value hypothesis): If, in the long run, I score more points on average.
5. **One assumption about my own workflow that this log proved wrong:** That I make my decisions rationally.
